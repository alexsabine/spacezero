The Invitation

A gathering at the edge

For three days in October, a small circle of thinkers, makers, and practitioners gathers on the far northern coast of California — where the redwoods meet the Pacific, in the waning days before the new moon.

Edge of the Moon is the autumn gathering in the Emergent series, hosted by Space Zero in Crescent City. It asks a simple question with deep roots: what becomes possible when we begin again from zero — when priors are loosened, attention is freed, and form is allowed to arise rather than be imposed?

Zero here is not emptiness as lack but emptiness as ground: the fertile dark from which a crescent of light first appears.

Beauty is the intrinsic order of things finding its own form. A working principle for the days ahead

A Flipped Gathering

The talks happen before we arrive

No lecterns. No passive afternoons. The thinking is shared in advance, so our time together is spent in the part that actually matters — the conversation.

Edge of the Moon runs as a flipped conference. The thinking is shared ahead of time as pre-recorded video; everyone watches before they travel. By the time we are in the same room we already share common ground — so every session can begin in the middle, where the real questions live.

Three Questions to Begin

What we are asking you to sit with

However you contribute — a delegate's introduction or a speaker's talk — let your thinking circle one or more of these. They are the gravitational centre of the gathering.

i

What becomes possible when we begin from zero — when a prior is set down rather than defended?

ii

Where have you seen form arise on its own — order, pattern, or beauty that no one imposed?

iii

What does your discipline know about beginnings that the others have yet to hear?

Delegates — a 5-minute introduction

Every delegate records a short 5-minute video introducing themselves — who you are, what you are working on, and what draws you to the theme. We watch these before arriving, so the first handshake already has a face and a thread to pull.

Speakers — a 45-minute pre-recording

Invited speakers prepare a 45-minute pre-recorded talk developing their thinking in full. Because it is watched in advance, the live session is given over entirely to discussion.

Hosted on our YouTube

Introductions and talks are gathered into a curated playlist on the Space Zero YouTube channel — open beforehand and lasting long after. Topics are welcome from across the sciences, contemplative traditions, the arts, and practice.

A keynote on the deepest version of the gathering's question — how shape, order, and pattern come to be in living matter, and what that tells us about goals and agency in the world. Speaker to be announced.

Adam Safron
On the mystery of consciousness

A tour of the hardest question — what experience is and how it could arise in a physical system — drawing on active inference and integrated world-modelling to ask what it would even mean to explain a mind.

Richard Silberstein
On creativity and the brain

What happens in the brain at the moment of insight — the dynamics of attention, emotion, and steady-state activity that underlie creative thought, and what the measurements reveal about how new ideas form.

Julian Gough
An evolutionary theory of the universe

The provocative case that our universe is less a dead machine than an evolving organism — descended, through cosmological natural selection, from earlier and simpler universes, and so disposed to develop, like an egg, rather than decay.

Online panel · hosted by Daniel Friedman
There is no self-evidence
A physics of emptiness realisation

Daniel Friedman hosts the authors — Lars Sandved-Smith, Chris Fields, Thomas Doctor, Ruben Laukkonen, and Jakob Hohwy — for a live discussion of a striking result: a persisting system gathers evidence for its own model, yet the very boundary that would separate self from world can never be evidenced from within. Every perception, then, is a complex system compressed into a way of seeing; the self is the one compression that cannot be confirmed.

Before You Arrive In preparation

A set of interactive simulations and short primers is in the works — drawn from frontier papers on consciousness, metaphysics, and the latest findings across fields. They are meant to make the key ideas, terms, and ways of knowing familiar before the gathering, so we can begin the conversation already fluent in one another's languages.

The Days

Three days, in the waning light

We meet in the quiet days before the new moon, as the light empties toward zero. The rhythm is unhurried and largely outdoors — walking tours, experiential sessions, and lectures held in the open air. Each evening, we share a meal.

The Place

Where the redwoods meet the sea

Crescent City sits on California's wild northern edge, where Redwood National and State Parks come down almost to the shore. Sea stacks rise from the Pacific; fog drifts through groves of thousand-year-old trees; the light at dusk is unlike anywhere else.

Space Zero is the host — a small, independent space for slow, serious, joyful enquiry. Numbers are deliberately limited so the circle stays close.
