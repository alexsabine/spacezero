Where Knowable Meets Unknowable
Crescent City · California

Space Zero is where wisdom practice and aesthetics meet scientific discovery; a place to pause, breathe, bridge, and build.

Held by the beauty of the Northern California coast, Space Zero offers ground for emptiness and fullness, from where new possibilities arise to the living field.

We call that ground the Zero State; where nature, as the origin of all forms and intelligence, quietly restores what has been forgotten.

In collaboration with change-makers in science, technology and contemplative traditions, we participate in building a future aligned across the full spectrum of intelligence.

Creative Advance for Future Flourishing

Four grounds of practice

01

Living in Aesthetics

As day softens, the ocean gathers the sun's last colours and returns them, wave by wave. Beauty is a way of being alive; through neuroaesthetics it opens the brain to creativity and fresh perspective.

02

Cultivate Embodied Wisdom

Like a redwood growing its rings, wisdom accrues in the body; values expressed as action. We help foster meaningful change through embodied practice.

03

Align as Nature

Alignment emerges. We attune our gathering and movement into the tides, the moon, the slow light. A school of fish tracks its neighbours, yet the whole turns and folds as one. We practise local attention for global impact.

04

Benevolence into Technology

Kindness gives technology its pulse — and pulse finds pulse, two rhythms drawn into time with one another. The builders of tomorrow are shaped by the ground they stand on. We tend that ground.
